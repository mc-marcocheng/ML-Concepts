<TLDR>
Overfitting gives low training error and poor validation error. Underfitting gives poor training and validation error.
</TLDR>

## Core idea

A model should learn patterns that generalize.

Overfitting occurs when a model learns training noise or spurious detail.

Underfitting occurs when a model is too simple or poorly trained.

## Error patterns

| Case | Training error | Validation error |
|---|---:|---:|
| Underfitting | High | High |
| Good fit | Low | Low |
| Overfitting | Low | High |

Use <a href="/learn/general-ml/cross-validation">Cross-Validation</a> to estimate validation performance.

## Causes

Overfitting can come from:

- too much model capacity
- too little data
- weak regularization
- data leakage
- too many training steps

Underfitting can come from:

- too little capacity
- too much regularization
- poor features
- too few training steps

## Controls

To reduce overfitting, use more data, stronger regularization, or early stopping.

To reduce underfitting, use a richer model, better features, or longer training.

See <a href="/learn/general-ml/bias-variance-tradeoff">Bias-Variance Tradeoff</a>.

## Common mistakes

Do not judge overfitting from training loss alone.

Do not tune repeatedly on the test set.

Do not treat a small train-validation gap as always good.
