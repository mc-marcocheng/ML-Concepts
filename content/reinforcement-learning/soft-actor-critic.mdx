<TLDR>
Soft Actor-Critic trains a stochastic policy to maximize reward plus entropy. It is off-policy and uses replay for data efficiency.
</TLDR>

## Core idea

SAC maximizes a maximum-entropy objective:

$$
\mathbb{E}
\left[
\sum_t
\gamma^t
(r_t+\alpha \mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot \mid s_t)))
\right].
$$

The entropy term rewards stochastic behavior.

The temperature $\alpha$ controls the entropy reward.

## Soft value target

SAC uses a soft action value.

A common target is:

$$
y
=
r+\gamma
\left[
Q_{\bar\phi}(s',a')
-\alpha \log \pi_\theta(a' \mid s')
\right].
$$

The action $a'$ comes from the current policy.

Target networks stabilize the critic update.

## Actor update

The actor minimizes:

$$
\mathbb{E}_{s,a \sim \pi_\theta}
[
\alpha \log \pi_\theta(a \mid s)-Q_\phi(s,a)
].
$$

This encourages high value and high entropy.

## Why SAC works well

SAC is off-policy.

It can reuse replay-buffer data.

Entropy helps exploration and reduces premature collapse.

See <a href="/learn/reinforcement-learning/exploration-vs-exploitation">Exploration vs Exploitation</a>.

## Common mistakes

Do not drop the entropy term when describing SAC.

Do not forget target networks for stable critic training.

Do not assume SAC is on-policy.
