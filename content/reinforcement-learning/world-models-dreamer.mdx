<TLDR>
A world model learns a compact predictive model of the environment. Dreamer trains an actor and critic on imagined rollouts in the learned latent space.
</TLDR>

## Core idea

A world model predicts how the environment changes.

It often uses latent states instead of raw observations.

A latent model can predict rewards, discounts, and future latent states.

Dreamer uses this learned model to imagine trajectories.

Then it trains the policy from those imagined trajectories.

## Dreamer components

Dreamer has three main parts.

1. A representation model encodes observations into latent states.

2. A dynamics model predicts future latent states.

3. Actor and critic networks learn from imagined rollouts.

The model does not need to reconstruct every pixel perfectly.

It must predict information useful for control.

## Imagined rollouts

Dreamer starts from real latent states.

It rolls the dynamics model forward under the actor.

It computes predicted rewards and values.

Then it updates the actor and critic through the imagined trajectory.

This improves data efficiency.

## Main risks

Learned dynamics can be wrong.

Long imagined rollouts can compound model errors.

The policy can exploit mistakes in the model.

Short horizons and model regularization can help.

## Link to other methods

Dreamer is model-based.

MuZero also plans in a learned latent space.

See <a href="/learn/reinforcement-learning/muzero">MuZero</a>.

## Common mistakes

Do not require a world model to reconstruct raw observations.

Do not trust long imagined rollouts without checking model error.

Do not confuse latent dynamics with observed-state dynamics.
