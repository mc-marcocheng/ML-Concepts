<TLDR>
MuZero learns a latent model that predicts rewards, values, and policies. It uses Monte Carlo tree search over the latent model for planning.
</TLDR>

## Core idea

MuZero combines learning and planning.

It does not require known environment dynamics.

It does not need to reconstruct observations.

It learns a latent model useful for search.

## Model parts

MuZero uses three learned functions.

The representation function maps an observation history to a latent state:

$$
h_0 = f_\theta(o_{\le t}).
$$

The dynamics function predicts the next latent state and reward:

$$
h_{k+1}, r_k = g_\theta(h_k,a_k).
$$

The prediction function outputs policy and value:

$$
p_k, v_k = p_\theta(h_k).
$$

## Planning

MuZero runs Monte Carlo tree search in latent space.

The search uses predicted rewards, values, and policies.

The improved search policy becomes a training target.

The value target comes from real rewards and search estimates.

## Why it matters

MuZero works without a hand-coded simulator.

It learns only what is needed for planning.

This separates it from models that reconstruct pixels.

See <a href="/learn/reinforcement-learning/world-models-dreamer">World Models and Dreamer</a>.

## Common mistakes

Do not say MuZero learns a full observation simulator.

Do not say it is purely model-free.

Do not ignore the role of tree search.
